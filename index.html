      • β 10Y — versus the CBOE 10-year yield index (^TNX). The beta is versus the percent change of that index, not versus one basis point of yield.
      • β DXY — versus the dollar index. β WTI — versus crude futures. β VIX — versus the VIX index.
      • Hot-CPI PnL — for a $1M position: (β10Y × 0.50 + βDXY × 1 + βVIX × 5) × $1,000,000. The label “10Y +50bp · DXY +1% · VIX +5pt” is the name of that shock. The arithmetic uses those three numbers (0.50, 1, 5) directly as multipliers on the betas.
      • Scenario PnL — the same multiplication, summed across all 10 names at $1M each. The base case adds a flat $500k with every shock at zero. Treat the dollars as this model’s scale, not a prime-broker revaluation.
      • Regime light — risk-on when VIX is under 20, neutral from 20 to 28, risk-off above 28. The 12 Oct VIX close sets the light.
      • Yield curve — the 10-year point is the 12 Oct ^TNX close. The 3-month, 2-year, 5-year, and 30-year points are fixed reference levels typed into the file, not a live curve.
      • Calendar — a typed list of known prints (FOMC minutes 15 Oct, and the other dates on the card). It is not pulled from a calendar feed.
